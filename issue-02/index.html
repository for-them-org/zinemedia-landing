Questions

What exactly am I getting?

A subscription to the Autostraddle print magazine. Issue #02 — King of Hearts, the food issue — ships to you now, pull-out poster and all. After that, every new issue lands on your doorstep as it comes out, four a year, 200+ pages each of essays, photoessays, reported features, fiction, poetry, comics, queer city guides, gay crosswords, astrology and advice. Your subscription also includes full AF+ digital membership — exclusive content on Autostraddle.com, member discounts on everything at For Them, and members-only newsletters.

Which issue arrives first?

Issue #02, King of Hearts — the food issue — it ships when you subscribe. After that, you'll get each new issue as it's published.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks from your account. No questions, no hidden fees, no auto-renewal traps.

What if I don't love my first issue?

Email us and we'll refund you. Keep the magazine either way.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — international shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee. Free shipping applies to US orders.

Who's behind it?

Autostraddle is one of the longest-running independent queer media outlets, published by For Them — both led by queer, trans, and lesbian editors, writers, and photographers.
