Questions

What exactly am I getting?

A subscription to the Autostraddle print magazine. The new issue — #03, the sports issue — ships to you now. After that, every new issue lands on your doorstep as it comes out, four a year, 200+ pages each of essays, photo essays, reported features, recipes, fiction, poetry, comics, queer city guides, gay crosswords, astrology and advice. Your subscription also includes full AF+ digital membership — exclusive content on Autostraddle.com, member discounts on everything at For Them, and members-only newsletters.

Which issue arrives first?

Issue #03 — it ships when you subscribe. Add the collection at checkout and Issues #00, #01 and #02 ship in the same box. Your next new issue is #04, this winter.

Can I get the back issues without subscribing?

Yes — each back issue is $30 on its own in the For Them shop. Subscribers get the new issue free and can add all three back issues to their first box for $90.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks from your account. No questions, no hidden fees, no auto-renewal traps.

What if I don't love my first issue?

Email us and we'll refund you. Keep the magazine either way.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — international shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee. Free shipping applies to US orders.

Who's behind it?

Autostraddle is one of the longest-running independent queer media outlets, published by For Them — both led by queer, trans, and lesbian editors, writers, and photographers.
